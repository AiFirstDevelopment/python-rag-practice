Brackwater Ferry: on board with pets

Dogs travel free. A dog must stay on a lead on the outer deck and is not allowed in the passenger lounge. Assistance
dogs are welcome everywhere on board. Cats and other small animals travel in a closed carrier, which may be taken
into the lounge.

A pet may stay in a vehicle during the crossing, with a window slightly open. Owners cannot go back to the car deck
once the ferry has left, so think about the weather before leaving an animal there. Water bowls stand by the doors
to the outer deck.

Luggage and lost property

Each foot passenger may bring two bags of up to 20 kilograms each at no charge. A third bag costs $8. Luggage is
left on the racks by the gangway doors and stays there for the crossing. Prams and wheelchairs are not counted as
bags.

Surfboards, kayaks and other long items go on the car deck and are booked as a bicycle would be: free, but booked.

Lost property is kept at the Port Ansel ticket office for 30 days. After that it is given to the charity shop on
Hollow Island. To ask about something lost, describe it and give the date and time of the sailing.

The crossing, food and getting around the boat

The crossing takes 55 minutes. Foot passengers board from 20 minutes before departure, and the gangway closes 5
minutes before departure. A passenger who reaches the gangway after it has closed has missed the sailing.

The cafe in the lounge serves hot drinks, soup and sandwiches. It takes cards only. Passengers may bring and eat
their own food. Alcohol is not sold and may not be drunk on board.

Wi-Fi is free, but it works only within about 15 minutes of either port; in the middle of the crossing there is no
signal. A lift runs between the car deck and the lounge. There are four wheelchair places in the lounge; tell the
crew at check-in and they will keep one.
