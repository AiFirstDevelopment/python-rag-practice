Brackwater Ferry: cars, motorcycles and bicycles

The car deck holds 38 cars. A car up to 5 metres long costs $48 one way, driver included. Each passenger in the car
pays the foot passenger fare. A motorcycle costs $22 one way, rider included. Bicycles travel free, but a bicycle
must be booked, because the rack holds only 12.

Vehicles must check in at least 30 minutes before departure. A vehicle that arrives later than that loses its place
and is put on standby for the next sailing with room. Standby vehicles are loaded in the order they arrived.

Drivers park where the crew points, put the handbrake on, leave the car in gear and switch off the alarm. Nobody
stays on the car deck during the crossing; the stairs and the lift lead up to the lounge.

Electric cars are welcome. There is no charging on board. The nearest chargers are in the Port Ansel car park and
beside the Hollow Island pier.

Tall vehicles, trailers and what a vehicle may carry

A vehicle over 2.1 metres high travels on the open part of the deck, where there are only six places, and must be
booked by phone. A trailer or a caravan is charged $6 per metre of length on top of the fare of the vehicle that
tows it. Motorhomes are charged as a car up to 5 metres and $6 per metre beyond that.

Spare fuel cans are not allowed in any vehicle, full or empty. Gas cylinders for camping are allowed, up to two for
each vehicle, with the valves closed. Fireworks are never carried.

Commercial freight is carried on the first sailing of the day from Monday to Friday and needs 48 hours' notice.
Livestock is carried by arrangement only.

A vehicle that breaks down on board is pushed ashore by the crew at no charge. Towing from the pier is the driver's
to arrange.
