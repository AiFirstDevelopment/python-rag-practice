Brackwater Ferry: fares and tickets

Brackwater Ferry sails between Port Ansel on the mainland and Hollow Island. This page lists what a crossing costs
for a passenger on foot. Vehicles are priced separately.

An adult foot passenger pays $14 one way and $24 for a return on the same day. Children aged 5 to 15 pay half the
adult fare. Children under 5 travel free, but they still need a ticket, which the ticket office issues at no charge.
Passengers aged 65 and over pay $10 one way on any sailing from Monday to Thursday. A group of ten or more adults
booked together gets one ticket in ten free.

Tickets are sold in three places: online, at the ticket office in Port Ansel, and at the machine on the Hollow
Island pier. The ticket office takes cards and cash. The machine takes cards only. Online tickets are sent by email
and can be shown on a phone, so there is nothing to print. A ticket is valid only for the sailing printed on it.

The ticket office in Port Ansel opens one hour before the first sailing of the day and closes when the last sailing
has left. There is no ticket office on Hollow Island.

Changing a ticket, refunds and the Islander Pass

A ticket can be moved to another sailing free of charge up to 2 hours before departure. After that, a change costs
$5 per ticket. Changes are made online or at the ticket office, not at the machine.

A ticket cancelled more than 24 hours before departure is refunded in full to the card that paid for it. A ticket
cancelled later than that is refunded as travel credit, which is valid for 12 months. No refund and no credit is
given for a sailing the passenger missed.

The Islander Pass gives residents of Hollow Island ten single crossings for $90. The pass is valid for 6 months from
the day it is bought and cannot be shared between people. Proof of an island address is checked when the pass is
bought, and the pass carries the holder's photograph.

A lost paper ticket is reprinted at the ticket office for $2. A lost Islander Pass is replaced for $10, with the
crossings that were left on it.
