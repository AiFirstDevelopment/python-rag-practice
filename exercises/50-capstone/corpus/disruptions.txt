Brackwater Ferry: weather and cancelled sailings

Sailings are cancelled when the wind at the harbour mouth stays above 40 knots. The decision is made 90 minutes
before departure. It is posted on the website and sent by text message to every passenger booked on that sailing.

If the company cancels a sailing, a ticket for it can be moved to any sailing in the next 7 days or refunded in
full, whichever the passenger chooses. The same holds for a vehicle booking. An Islander Pass crossing used for a
cancelled sailing is put back on the pass.

Fog does not cancel a sailing, but it slows the boat: allow up to 30 minutes more for the crossing. In a heavy swell
the outer deck is closed and the cafe may stop serving hot drinks.

Late sailings, missed connections and the winter timetable

If a sailing leaves more than 60 minutes late, every passenger gets a voucher for a free hot drink. If it leaves
more than 3 hours late, half the fare is refunded. The company does not pay for a train, bus or flight missed
because of a late or cancelled sailing.

From November to March there are four sailings a day in each direction instead of seven. The last sailing of the
day then leaves Port Ansel at 18:10 and Hollow Island at 19:20.

For two weeks in January the ferry is out of the water for its yearly inspection. A smaller boat runs in its place
and carries foot passengers and bicycles only, so no cars cross during those two weeks.
